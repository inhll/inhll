What we do

Research infrastructure, not research consultancy.

Most product teams don't need a full-time researcher — they need a system. Sibling Systems embeds a high-velocity research practice into your team, builds the operational infrastructure to keep it running, and train our replacements to deliver fast, reliable user insights when decisions are on the line.

How Rolling Research works

01

Lightweight intake

Teams submit research questions through a simple Google Form. No lengthy briefs. No kickoff overhead. Just a clear question and a timeline.

02

Weekly triage

We review and prioritize requests every Friday, kick off projects on Mondays, and run moderated sessions throughout the week. Most projects complete in two weeks or less.

03

Flash Findings

Outputs are concise, actionable documents — an executive summary and direct answers to your core questions. No 60-slide decks. Just what you need to move.

04

Embedded, not extracted

We stay close to your team through Slack, share work-in-progress insights, and surface exemplary sessions so even busy stakeholders stay connected to users.

05

AI Accelerated

We understand where AI speeds things up and where humans need to stay in the loop. Research outputs are structured and dated so your AI and RAG systems can ingest them — keeping your assistant's knowledge current as users and markets move.

06

Learning First

Our instructional design background perfectly prepares us to help your team learn key processes, build adoption plans, assessment rubrics, and teach your team to learn to learn from users.

AI Ground Truth Audit

Most companies shipping AI assistants have no process for verifying that what the AI believes about their users and policies is still true — no owner, no cadence, no accountability for the corpus. That gap is what the AI Ground Truth Audit is designed to close.

We inventory everything feeding your AI (help docs, personas, research repos, policy documents, support tickets, AI-generated summaries), date-stamp it, verify that the knowledge your retrieval layer draws from matches current reality, flag AI-feeding-AI recursion loops, and map drift risk by decision exposure. Deliverable: a Flash Findings report with a prioritized fix list and recommended cadence design.

Audit fee credited 100% toward a Setup Sprint if signed within 60 days.
