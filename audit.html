AI Ground Truth Audit

How stale is the user knowledge feeding your AI?

Your AI is only as accurate as your freshest user data. The Audit is a two-week, fixed-scope diagnostic that tells you exactly how fresh — or how stale — the knowledge feeding your AI actually is, and where that staleness is most likely to hurt.

1 · Corpus inventory

We catalogue every source feeding your AI's understanding of users: help docs, personas, research repos, support tickets. We date-stamp each source — verified this quarter, this year, or unknown — so you can see, at a glance, how much of what your AI "knows" about users has actually been checked against reality recently.

2 · Drift-risk map

We rank stale beliefs by decision exposure. Where is outdated user knowledge most likely to produce a bad product decision, a legal liability, or a confidently wrong assistant response? The highest-exposure gaps come first, so your fixes follow the risk.

3 · Recursion check

We flag AI-feeding-AI loops: meeting notes summarized by a bot, AI-generated personas passed back to the model, synthetic research used to train internal tools. This is the mechanism behind model collapse — happening quietly inside your own walls.

4 · Deliverable

A Flash Findings report: executive summary, prioritized fix list, and a recommended research cadence design. The fix list is the scope of a Setup Sprint — and the audit fee is credited 100% toward a Sprint signed within 60 days.
